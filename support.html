FAQ

What services are supported?

NFlux NZB supports SABnzbd+ and compatible Newznab/NZB indexer services that you configure yourself.

Does NFlux NZB host or provide content?

No. NFlux NZB is a remote management client for user-configured services. It does not host, index, sell, or provide downloadable content.

Where is my configuration stored?

Server addresses, API keys, RSS feeds, and preferences are stored locally on your device.

Why can I not connect to my server?

Check the server URL, API key, local network or VPN access, and whether your SABnzbd+ or indexer API is reachable from your iPhone.
