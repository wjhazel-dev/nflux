Privacy Policy

NFlux NZB does not collect, store, sell, or share personal information.

Local Configuration

Server addresses, API keys, RSS feeds, and configuration information are stored locally on the user's device. This information is used only to connect to services the user configures.

Network Requests

NFlux NZB connects directly to user-configured SABnzbd+ servers and compatible NZB indexer services. NFlux NZB does not route those requests through Hazel Media servers.

Content

NFlux NZB does not host content, provide download services, or operate an NZB indexer. It is a remote management client for services configured by the user.

Analytics and Crash Reporting

NFlux NZB does not currently include third-party analytics or crash reporting. If analytics or crash reporting services are added in the future, this policy may be updated.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact nflux@hazelmedia.co.
